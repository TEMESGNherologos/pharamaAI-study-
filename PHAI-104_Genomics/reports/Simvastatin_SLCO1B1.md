## Clinical Recommendations by Phenotype

### Normal function
Standard simvastatin dosing up to 40 mg/day.

### Decreased function
Avoid simvastatin >20 mg/day. Consider rosuvastatin/pravastatin.

### Poor function
Avoid simvastatin. Use rosuvastatin (SLCO1B1-independent).
