## Clinical Recommendations by Phenotype

### PM
Consider aromatase inhibitor (anastrozole) for post-menopausal patients.

### IM
Avoid CYP2D6 inhibitors (paroxetine, fluoxetine). Higher tamoxifen dose (40mg) may be considered.

### NM
Standard tamoxifen 20 mg/day.

### UM
Standard dosing — possible increased endoxifen levels.
